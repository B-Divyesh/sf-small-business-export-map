Plain-language terms

A preflight tool, not accounting advice.

Export Map reshapes CSV data according to instructions you choose. It does not interpret tax meaning, categorise records, or guarantee acceptance by every accounting system.

Your responsibility

Review the preview and handoff manifest before sharing an export. Keep your original file. You are responsible for selecting the correct column mappings, number separators, and date formats requested by your recipient.

One-time purchase

Export Map Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited saved recipient profiles and profile backup on this product. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. Checkout, payment support, and refunds are handled there. A refunded or revoked license will stop unlocking Pro features.

Availability

The software is provided “as is” without warranties. We may update the local app and verification service. Core CSV preflight and export remain available without payment.

Acceptable use

Do not use the product to process data you are not authorised to handle or to violate applicable law.
