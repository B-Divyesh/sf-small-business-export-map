Privacy

Your records stay on this device.

Export Map reads CSV files in your browser. It does not upload CSV contents, previews, or downloads to us.

What is stored

Recipient profiles are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. A license token and its most recent verification result are stored in localStorage when you unlock the one-time purchase. You can delete profiles in the app or clear this site’s browser data.

Network requests

The app makes no analytics or advertising requests. When a license is present, it may contact the Sociobot billing API once per day to confirm that license. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, and their policies apply there.

Your files

Uploaded CSV files are read into the current browser tab. The original is never modified. Downloads are created locally. Closing or refreshing the tab clears the active file.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
