Privacy

Your records stay on this device.

Export Map reads CSV files on your device. It does not send CSV contents, previews, or downloads to us.

What is stored

Recipient profiles are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. A license token and its latest check are stored in localStorage after purchase. Delete profiles in the app, or clear this site’s browser data.

Network requests

The CSV workflow makes only same-origin requests. Export Map has no analytics or advertising. With a license, it can contact the Sociobot billing API once per day. Checkout opens on Sociobot/Dodo.

Your files

The original file is never modified. Downloads are created on your device. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the active file.

Contact

Email privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.
